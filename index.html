      Patient guide

      Your visit, step by step

      1. Talk or book

        Start a conversation with your doctor here, or call to book.

      2. Registration

        Our front desk opens your patient file in a few minutes.

      3. Consultation & X-ray

        Your specialist examines you. If imaging is needed, it is done in-house.

      4. Medicines & plan

        Collect medicines at our pharmacy and get your follow-up date.

      Please bring

      • Old X-rays, scans and reports
      • Your current medicines or their strips
      • Inhalers, for chest consultations
      • Photo ID and mobile number

      Call 108 straight away if

      • A bone looks bent, or someone cannot move after an accident
      • Severe breathlessness, blue lips or chest pain
      • Coughing up blood

      Questions

      Common questions

      Do I need an appointment?

      You can visit during OPD hours. Booking ahead reduces your waiting time.

      Can I get my X-ray done at the hospital?

      Yes. Our in-house radiology unit is open every day from 8 AM to 5 PM.

      Is there a pharmacy?

      Yes. Our pharmacy is open every day from 8 AM to 5 PM, and also stocks inhalers and orthopaedic supports.

      How does online consultation work?

      Book a weekend slot between 2 PM and 6 PM. Our front desk calls you to confirm and shares the video call details.

      Is the symptom explorer a diagnosis?

      No. It shows conditions that commonly match your symptoms, so you know what to ask. Only an examination by the doctor can confirm the cause.

      How do I book on WhatsApp?

      Fill in the booking steps on this site and tap "Send on WhatsApp". The request opens in WhatsApp with all your details ready to send to our front desk.

      Can I install this website as an app?

      Yes. On Android, open the menu in Chrome and choose "Install app" or "Add to Home screen". On iPhone, tap Share and choose "Add to Home Screen".

      Can the doctor explain in Telugu?

      Yes. Consultations are in Telugu or English.
